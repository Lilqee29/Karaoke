## 🎬 Recording Tips

1. **Screen record** on your phone (iPhone: Settings > Control Center > Screen Recording)
2. **Use 0.5x zoom** in GameBoy to show more content
3. **Record each scene separately**, then edit together
4. **Add captions** — most people watch without sound
5. **Keep text on screen for 2-3 seconds** minimum
6. **Use transitions** — swipe, zoom, or cut between scenes
